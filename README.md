## Six Words, One Story

One complete story in exactly six words:

> Came home early. She wasn't alone.

`main.py` scores a shortlist of candidate six-word stories against the
judge brief — a complete story, a turn or surprise, every word earning
its place, clean rhythm aloud, something left unsaid — and writes the
winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
